# Persona roadmap — Group 14: Bookkeeper/auditor/reporting user (701-750)

Branch `feat/persona-group4-rest`, PR #809. Surveyed 2026-11-24 at `7ea275b75`. Read-only survey; nothing built, nothing changed.

Status: EXISTS = shipped before this batch (evidence given); PARTIAL = usable in part, needs extension; BUILD = not present in usable form. `verified` marks evidence checked firsthand in this survey.

Bookkeeper domain as found: the persona is three surfaces on one identity — (a) the statement reader (`/statements`, `client/src/pages/food/StatementCenterPage.jsx`, 156 lines: per-product readiness across the five statement products, personal/work account groups, scope picker, embedded preview/archive, search; `GET /api/statements/center`, `server/routes/statementCenter.js:22`, scoped server-side to `req.user.id`); (b) the statement archive (`client/src/pages/food/StatementArchivePage.jsx`, 545 lines: `GET /archive`, `/:documentId`, `/download`, `/link`, `server/routes/statementArchive.js`, append-only issued originals, ready-only artifacts, short-lived signed URLs); (c) the investor reporter (`client/src/pages/InvestorDashboardPage.jsx`, 534 lines: KPIs, revenue chart, franchise pipeline, supply/demand, evidence drill-through; `GET /api/investor/*`, `server/routes/investor.js`, gated on `investor.reporting` plus a per-dataset audience check). Underneath: `server/services/statementCenterService.js` (explicit nulls for missing data, never reassuring zeros), `server/services/statementSearchService.js` (unified search across all five products, amount-aware, saved views), `server/services/statementExportJobService.js` + `server/services/statementExportService.js` (export jobs, bulk ZIP, accounting OFX/QFX/QBO), `server/routes/statementInvestigation.js` (entry drilldown, cases, statement-level reports, correction diffs), `server/routes/statementDisputeMessages.js` (owner-or-admin follow-up thread on disputes), `server/routes/agentCreditAdjustments.js` (admin-only adjustment review), `server/routes/invest.js` (Invest opportunities/commitments, live gate off), `server/services/access/accessService.js` (`listOwnGrants`, investor audience projection).

Two scope facts the whole group turns on: the statement surface is genuinely shipped end to end (701-705, 711-714, 738-739, 749 are EXISTS), but the audit-coordination surface (716-720, 741-745) is mostly missing — there is no audit request to coordinate around, and the one intake that exists for statement problems (`statement_reports`) has no consumer anywhere in the repo (defect 1). And the investor reporting surface is read-only by design (D-408) but fails open where the statement surface fails closed: KPI queries return zero-values on missing tables with no warning (defect 2), while the statement center marks missing data explicitly null.

Money note: unlike Group 11 (D-257: rental money off-platform, record-only), Group 12 (franchise money record-only per D-291), and Group 13 (money moves on-platform per D-353), Group 14 money is READ-ONLY: no item 701-750 is itself a money-moving action, and the reporting surfaces never move money. The items sit on top of money paths that do move on-platform — restaurant wallet payouts (747: Stripe Transfer via `restaurant_payouts`, fail-closed on the credit rail), business refund requests (748: Money Approvals queue, requester never executes), Invest commitments (732-735: live gate `INVEST_COMMITMENTS_ENABLED` default off) — and the build must keep those gates intact (rail kill-switch on fiat payouts, two-person approval on refunds and distributions, commitment gate off). Decision D-408 records the money model.

## Pre-existing defects spotted (not fixed — list only)

1. **`statement_reports` is write-only.** The investigation router inserts statement-level problem reports (`server/routes/statementInvestigation.js:1088`) and lists them back to the filer (`:1105`), but no admin route, service, or worker anywhere in the repo reads `statement_reports` — no queue, no review, no notice. A filed statement problem currently goes nowhere. (Bears on 716 and 745.)
2. **Investor KPIs fail open to zeros.** `server/controllers/investorController.js` ("All queries fail open — missing tables return zero-values", `safeQuery`/`safeInt`) renders a failed or missing ledger as revenue 0, 0 users, 0 jobs with no warning — the opposite of the statement center's explicit-null policy (`statementCenterService.js:14-18`). A bookkeeper cannot distinguish "no revenue" from "ledger unreadable". (Bears on 722.)
3. **Franchise pipeline labels fall through to raw slugs.** `InvestorDashboardPage.jsx` `PipelineRow` maps only `pending`/`reviewing`/`approved`/`activated`/`rejected`, but the API returns `inquiry`/`screening`/`application_review`/`diligence`/`agreement_pending`/`approved`/`launched`/`rejected`/`archived` (`investorController.js` `getFranchisePipeline`) — seven of nine stages render their raw status slug instead of a label.
