# Persona roadmap — Group 13: Credit participant/cashier (651-700)

Branch `feat/persona-group4-rest`, PR #809. Surveyed 2026-11-24 at `a4ac014fe`. Read-only survey; nothing built, nothing changed.

Status: EXISTS = shipped before this batch (evidence given); PARTIAL = usable in part, needs extension; BUILD = not present in usable form. `verified` marks evidence checked firsthand in this survey.

Credit domain as found: the persona is three surfaces on one identity — (a) the Credit participant who applies to accept Credit (`credit-participant` signup persona, `client/src/utils/signupPersonas.js:18`, intent `join_credit`, `server/services/access/signupIntent.js:40`, status path `/credit/apply`); (b) the customer wallet holder (`client/src/pages/food/OxsteedCreditPage.jsx`, 1171 lines: balance strip, holds, statement, disputes, limits, transfers, withdrawals, in-store authorization); (c) the kiosk cashier (`client/src/pages/food/FoodKioskPage.jsx`, device-authenticated `server/routes/oxsteedCreditKiosk.js`, `POST /redeem` + `POST /cash-out`, card + PIN). Underneath: `server/routes/oxsteedCredit.js` (account, limits, status, holds, eligibility, identity-verification, statement, transactions, disputes, top-ups, withdrawals, cards), `server/services/oxsteedCreditService.js` (eligibility, limits, holds, statement reconciliation, disputes, settlement), `server/services/oxsteedCreditParticipantService.js` (546 lines: apply/enroll/activate only — no suspend/revoke path), `server/services/oxsteedCreditCardService.js` + `server/services/oxsteedCreditCardRecovery.js` (cards, freeze, lost/found, replacement, history), `server/services/oxsteedCreditTopupService.js` + `server/controllers/oxsteedCreditTopupController.js` (self top-ups + business-funded member top-ups, G-22), `server/controllers/restaurantTopupController.js` (restaurant top-up eligibility + intents), `server/routes/reconciliation.js` + `server/controllers/reconciliationController.js` (admin-only exception workspace), `server/services/oxsteedCreditDisputeService.js` (disputes, provisional credit, SLA notices), `server/middleware/checkRestrictions.js` (suspension/restriction explanations on blocked money actions).

Two scope facts the whole group turns on: the participant control plane (`credit_participants`, migrations 724/725) has an activation path only — apply, admin enroll, maker-checker activation gated on Didit — and no suspend/revoke transition anywhere in the service; partner suspension (`transitionPartner`, `server/services/oxsteedCreditAdminService.js:1125`) never touches `credit_participants`. And the reconciliation workspace (686-690) is admin-only by route and by page; there is no cashier- or location-facing reconciliation surface at all — the customer statement reconciliation (660) is the only participant-visible reconciliation, and it is per-account, not per-location.

Money note: unlike Group 11 (D-257: rental money off-platform, record-only) and Group 12 (franchise money record-only per D-291), Group 13 money DOES move on-platform: Stripe-funded top-ups (`oxsteedCreditTopupService.js`, top-up statuses `pending`/`completed`/`failed`/`requires_attention`, migration 633), withdrawals to bank, customer transfers, kiosk card redemptions and kiosk cash-out (physical cash dispensed), settlement payouts. Everything is fail-closed on the rail gates and on `checkRestrictions('checkout')`. Known open sharp item, not re-decided here: the live top-up lot-creation actor bug (top-up service posts actor `system`, migration 487 trigger raises unless actor is admin with user id + reason) is still open from the Nov 17 Invest notes. No Group 13 item is itself a new money-moving action except insofar as 666-670 (checkout) and 676-680 (top-ups) extend existing money paths; the build must keep the rail gates and the two-person settlement approval intact. Decision D-353 records the money model.

## Pre-existing defects spotted (not fixed — survey only)

1. **Participant status never follows partner status.** `oxsteedCreditParticipantService.js` has apply/enroll/activate only — no suspend/revoke transition exists anywhere in `server/` (outside tests). `transitionPartner` (`server/services/oxsteedCreditAdminService.js:1125`) never touches `credit_participants`. A suspended or terminated partner leaves its participant row `active`, so the apply page keeps showing the participant as active and the participant keeps its allowed surfaces. (Basis for 700 PARTIAL.)
2. **Granular restriction reason is fetched and dropped.** `server/middleware/checkRestrictions.js` selects `user_restrictions.reason` but the `ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED` 403 names only the blocked action and the expiry; the suspension path in the same file does include `suspended_reason`. (Basis for 693 PARTIAL.)
3. **Transaction history is posted-only.** `listCustomerCreditTransactions` filters `WHERE t.status = 'posted'` (`server/services/oxsteedCreditService.js:3471`), so in-flight activity (a pending top-up load, a held amount not yet captured) never appears in activity — only the holds panel shows pending reservations. (Basis for 657 PARTIAL.) Slice 4b completed 657 via the pending-top-ups panel and left the posted-only list as-is, so this defect stays open as its own item.
4. **No notification on platform-initiated card freeze.** `transitionCardStatus` (`server/services/oxsteedCreditCardService.js`) makes no notification call; a customer whose card is frozen or revoked by the platform learns it only from the status label on the cards page. (Basis for 665 PARTIAL.)
5. **Identity state is invisible on the wallet page.** `OxsteedCreditPage.jsx` has no identity-verification section (only step-up code inputs for withdrawals/transfers); the tier, latest-verification, and appeal path live only on the identity-verification endpoints and `VerifyIdentityPage`. A participant whose Didit verification lapses gets no in-wallet pointer to re-verify. (Related to 653/695.)
