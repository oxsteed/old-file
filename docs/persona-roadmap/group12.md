Two franchise domains exist and must not be confused: (a) the OxSteed territory franchise above (the operator persona's own pipeline); (b) the restaurant-brand franchise in `server/routes/restaurantBrands.js` (`/:brandId/franchise-report`, `/:brandId/franchise-mappings`) and `client/src/pages/food/BrandHQPanel.jsx` (brand compliance, store comparison, royalty report, menu publish/rollouts), plus the restaurant training/audit system in `server/routes/restaurants.js:347-399`. Many PARTIAL rows below rest on reusing (b) or the generic business/support systems; the build must decide whether the operator surface is territory-scoped or reuses those restaurant/business surfaces.

Money note: unlike Group 11 (D-257: rental money off-platform, record-only), franchise money has NO decision yet. The machinery exists server-side — initial-fee collection is recorded, never moved (`recordFranchiseFeeCollection`, `franchiseController.js:1184`); royalties accrue as `platform_ledger` bookkeeping (`franchiseRoyaltyService.js:33`, "No money moves here"); activation fails closed until the fee is collected or waived (`franchiseController.js:1184-1258`, `activateFranchiseStore`). No item 601-650 is itself a money-moving action; the fee/royalty model decision is still open (see Next step).

Build 604 (interview scheduling: needs a call — is it a consulting-style booking or a dedicated HQ interview slot?) first, then 603 (applicant-visible checklist detail + document upload; the 8 HQ process items are not applicant documents, so the document set needs defining) and 605 (applicant-visible follow-up: surface `shared` notes or a reply thread; the write side already exists).

Then 606 (operator accept/decline step — needs a call: is admin approval the assignment, or does the operator accept?), 607 (boundary definition — needs a call: what defines a boundary — city/region strings, an explicit area list, or a map?), 609 (launch plan surface on top of the readiness status), 610 (operator-facing assignment history from the existing activity log — cheapest item in the cluster).

**Money-moving items (fail-closed gating needed at build time):** no item 601-650 is itself a money-moving action, but the cluster sits on top of franchise money machinery that has no model decision yet (unlike D-257 for rentals — flag as needing one, do not make it). Money-adjacent, in build order: 601 (the agreement sets the initial fee, royalty bps, and marketing-fee bps — `FranchiseHQPage.jsx:271-276`); 609/613/615 (launch is fail-closed on fee collection-or-waiver and training completion — `activateFranchiseStore`, `franchiseController.js:1258-1458`); 626 (the performance rollup reads `platform_ledger` revenue and the royalty accruals — read-only, but it is the only operator-visible money surface). Underneath: `recordFranchiseFeeCollection` records a collection (or waiver) with a reference, never moves money (`franchiseController.js:1184`); `franchiseRoyaltyService.js` accrues royalties to the owner's ledger and marketing fees to the agreement as bookkeeping ("No money moves here", `:33`); royalty failures park for admin review (`GET /api/admin/super/franchise-royalty-failures`). Before any fee/royalty surface is built for the operator, the coordinator needs the franchise money-model decision: on-platform movement vs record-only (D-257 pattern) vs something else — including whether the royalty ledger credit is a payout promise.

**Ambiguous scope:**
- 603 Document checklist vs intake checklist: the seeded 8 items are HQ process steps, not applicant documents. The build must define the document set (identity, entity, financials?) separately.
- 606 Territory acceptance: is admin approval the assignment, or does the operator accept? No accept step exists; the grant mints on launch.
- 607 Assignment boundary: a territory is currently city/region/country strings. What counts as a boundary — strings, an explicit area list, or a map? Decides 637 too.
- 611 vs 609 vs 613: launch milestones, launch plan, and readiness review overlap; confirm they stay three items and which one owns the dates.
- 612 vs 616-617: opening team roster vs territory invitations / manager assignments overlap; confirm the roster is the team being invited/assigned, not a second list.
- 620 Territory announcement feed: HQ broadcast to operators, or an activity feed of territory events? The activity log (610) already records events admin-side.
- 626 "Permitted" performance rollup: "permitted" reads as territory-scoped (like 599's "permitted" earnings) — the rollup is scoped, but helpers/jobs match by city/region string, so "permitted" is best-effort, not enforced.
- 628/631/632-635 exception cluster: two candidate owners (support tickets vs restaurant audits). Pick one before building, or the cluster ships twice.
- 636-640 planning cluster: are candidate locations new franchise territories, or restaurant/business locations inside a territory? The HQ list tracks territories; `franchise_location_agreements` maps launched stores. The build must name the entity.
- 641-645 transition: operator-initiated handoff vs HQ-driven reassignment (admin `owner_user_id` change + grant revocation already exist). The handoff flow differs completely between the two.
- Two franchise domains: many PARTIALs reuse the restaurant-brand franchise (BrandHQ, training, audits) or generic business/support systems. The build must decide per item whether the operator surface is territory-scoped or reuses those surfaces — reusing the restaurant audit system for a territory operator is a product call, not a given.
