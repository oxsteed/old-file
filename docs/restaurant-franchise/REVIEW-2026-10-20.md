## 1. Executive summary

### Overall verdict in plain words

**Not yet franchise grade.** OxSteed Restaurants is a capable single-restaurant ordering and operations app with an unusually strong "operating discipline" layer: checklists, SOPs, training, quality audits, inventory basics, kitchen stations, managed screens and audited platform-admin access. That layer delivers on the strategy promise of running an independent restaurant "with the structure of a major franchise system."

The transaction core that chains judge first is not at that level yet. That core is how you get paid, how the numbers are reported, which hours apply and how the store keeps working offline. Several confirmed bugs touch money and hours today. And the product has **no real multi-location or brand model**. Locations exist as address labels, but menus, prices, 86ing, inventory, staff, permissions, tax, hours, printers and reports all live at the restaurant (brand) level. A franchisee group or a multi-unit operator cannot run several stores the way Toast or Square customers do.

So:

- **Single independent restaurant:** mostly usable, once the P0 and P1 items below are fixed.
- **Multi-unit or franchise operator:** not ready. The missing brand/location hierarchy is a re-architecture, not a patch.

### What is already strong

These strengths are worth keeping as the base:

- **Tenant isolation.** Every `:restaurantId` route runs `requireRestaurantAccess()` or carries a reviewed `scopeExempt(...)` marker, and `routeScopeGuard` enforces this (`server/middleware/scopeGates.js:85-132`). Platform-admin access fails closed unless it is audit-logged (`scopeGates.js:102-125`).
- **Online order idempotency.** `Idempotency-Key` is required and fingerprinted, and it is re-checked after the row lock (`foodController.js:2904-3007`).
- **Kitchen lifecycle.** Item-level states, station routing, quality-check gating and expo are in place (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:998-1054`, `/kitchen/*` routes).
- **POS integrity on the server.** Split and merge are blocked when payments exist, refunds are capped at the paid amount, refunds and stored value require an idempotency key, cash over/short is computed at drawer close, and voids are manager-only (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:976-996, 3233-3350, 4518-4665`).
- **Inventory.** Reservations are made at order time, stock cannot go negative, and every change is audited.
- **Operating discipline.** Checklists, SOPs, training with quizzes, quality audits with corrective actions, crew tasks with photo proof.
- **Managed screens v2.** Revisioned sync, offline drafts and a cross-tab lock (`docs/restaurant-screens/implementation.md`).
- **Locales.** `food.*` (1,143 keys), `screens.*` and `restaurants.*` are present in all five required locales, with no missing keys.

## 3. Detailed findings

Each finding lists: **severity**, **evidence** (file:line), **user impact**, and **fix direction**. No fixes were implemented.

### P0

#### F-01: Restaurant time zone is UTC for every web-created restaurant (P0)

**Evidence**

- **Both web creation paths store UTC:**
  - `foodController.createRestaurant` inserts `normalizeString(req.body.timezone, 80) || 'UTC'` (`server/controllers/foodController.js:2438`).
  - The web onboarding form never sends a time zone (`client/src/pages/food/FoodMerchantPage.jsx:56-83`).
  - The Business Hub wizard path `ensureDraftFoodRestaurantForBusiness` does not copy `businesses.timezone` at all (`server/controllers/businessController.js:50-68`).
  - The column default is `'UTC'`.
- **Nothing on the web can change it.** No client page under `client/src/pages/food` sends or shows `timezone` (grep finds nothing). Only the native `updatePublishing` endpoint can set it (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:1873-1889`).
- **Everything time-based uses this value:**
  - Open/closed status and order acceptance: `evaluateOperatingHours(restaurant.operating_hours, restaurant.timezone)` (`foodController.js:704, 3044`).
  - Daily queue-number reset (`foodController.js:3390`; `restaurantAppFlowController.js:3716-3723, 4030-4044`).
  - Manager reports by day (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:1650`).
- **Reproduced:** `evaluateOperatingHours({tue:11:00–22:00}, 'UTC', Tue 10:30 New York)` returns open. With `America/New_York` it returns closed. A UTC store with 11:00–22:00 hours is therefore open 7 AM–6 PM Eastern.

**User impact**

- Guests are blocked from ordering during the real dinner service and allowed to order when the store is closed.
- Queue numbers restart at 8 PM ET (UTC midnight) in the middle of dinner.
- Day-based reports split sales across the wrong days.

**Fix direction**

- Require a valid IANA time zone at onboarding: default it from the browser (`Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`) or derive it from coordinates, and let the owner confirm it.
- Copy `businesses.timezone` in the Business Hub path.
- Add a time-zone field to the web settings.
- Backfill existing `UTC` restaurants from their coordinates or address, with an owner confirmation prompt.
- Validate time zones on write (see F-10).

### P1

#### F-02: No brand → location hierarchy; locations are labels only (P1)

**Evidence**

- **One restaurant per business:** `idx_food_restaurants_business_unique ON food_restaurants(business_id)` (verified in the migrated DB). A group of stores is therefore either one restaurant with "locations", or unrelated businesses with no parent.
- **`restaurant_locations` holds only address, hours and time zone** (`server/migrations/319_restaurant_locations.sql`).
- **Orders gained `location_id` only in migration 887** (`887_restaurant_screen_sync.sql:4`), and it is set only for managed-screen and device orders (`foodController.js:2887, 3564-3566`). Online, POS and kiosk orders on the main paths have no location.
- **Everything else is keyed by `restaurant_id` only:** menu items (`food_menu_items` has no location columns), the availability/86 flag, inventory quantities, staff membership and permissions (`370_restaurant_workspace_permissions.sql` grants restaurant-wide), tax rate, service fee, operating hours, printer settings (one row per restaurant, `912_restaurant_printer_settings.sql`), and all reports (`restaurantReportsController.js`, `getManagerReports`).
- **Online ordering checks the restaurant's hours, not a location's** (`foodController.js:3044`).
- **Inventory "transfers" move stock between storage areas inside one restaurant, not between stores** (`restaurantInventoryOperationsController.js:503-509`).
- **The code's "franchise" module is OxSteed's own territory-franchise program, not chain management for restaurants:** `franchise_programs`, applications and territories (`247_franchise_programs.sql`, `franchiseController.js`), plus royalties (`franchiseRoyaltyService.js`).

**User impact**

- A multi-unit operator cannot set prices or 86 an item at one store, give a manager access to only their store, keep stock per store, use per-store tax jurisdictions, or compare stores.
- HQ cannot push a menu, training or audits to many stores, or roll up sales.

**Fix direction**

- Introduce `brand` (or `organization`) → `store` (location) as first-class tenancy:
  - `location_id` NOT NULL on orders, payments, shifts, inventory, devices and printers.
  - Location-scoped memberships and roles (HQ admin, area manager, store manager, staff).
  - A brand menu with per-store override tables (price, availability, tax category).
  - Location-aware reports with a brand roll-up.
- Plan it as a staged migration: default every existing restaurant to a single primary store, then cut over each surface.

#### F-03: Cashiers cannot take payment; money RBAC is hard-coded to owner/manager (P1)

**Evidence**

- **Server:**
  - `recordPosPayment` calls `assertManage(restaurant)` for every manual payment type: cash, external/mobile, wallet credit, OxSteed credit and refunds (`server/controllers/restaurantAppFlowController.js:4538-4542`).
  - `assertManage` checks `MANAGE_ROLES = owner|manager` and ignores the team-permission catalog and per-membership overrides (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:833-837`).
- **Client:** the POS hides payment from non-managers, with "Recording a POS payment moves real money, so the pay flow is manager-only" (`client/src/pages/food/RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:262-265, 377-380`).
- **Same pattern elsewhere:** separate hard-coded `MANAGE_ROLES` sets in reports (`restaurantReportsController.js:13`), ops (`restaurantOpsController.js:43`), audits (`restaurantAuditsController.js:15`), training (`restaurantTrainingController.js:13`), runner (`restaurantRunnerController.js:83`) and printer (`restaurantPrinterController.js:21`). Other routes use the permission catalog (`foodController.js:1704`). So a custom "Shift lead" role with `pos.manage` can do some things but cannot take cash.

**User impact**

- At the counter, a manager must be present for every sale.
- Accountability is blurred: payments land in whichever open drawer the manager picks (`RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:266-269`).

**Fix direction**

- Let any staff member with `pos.use` tender cash, card and mobile **within their own open drawer shift**.
- Keep manager approval (PIN or override) for refunds, voids, discounts above a threshold and no-sale opens.
- Replace the scattered `MANAGE_ROLES` checks with one permission service (catalog plus overrides, and later location scope).

#### F-04: Web POS checkout is two non-atomic, non-idempotent requests (P1)

**Evidence**

- **Pay sends two calls:** `POST /pos/orders`, then `POST /pos/orders/:id/payments` (`RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:402-417`).
- **No idempotency on the order:**
  - `clientOrderId` is never sent (`RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:383-401`).
  - `createPosOrder` has no replay lookup. `clientOrderId` is only stored in metadata and used for the receipt-audit key (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:3737, 3841-3843`).
  - The payment idempotency key is a fresh `crypto.randomUUID()` on every attempt (`RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:415`).
- **The order has already gone to the kitchen:** printer jobs and the broadcast are queued at order creation (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:3822, 3848`).

**User impact**

- If the payment call fails (decline, credit hold error, network), the ticket is already in the kitchen unpaid.
- Pressing Pay again creates a second order: duplicate food, and a voided ticket the manager must clean up.

**Fix direction**

- Send a stable `clientOrderId` (an `Idempotency-Key` held for the whole checkout) and replay on it in `createPosOrder`, as the online path does.
- On Pay: either reuse the order already created and only retry the payment, or provide a single "create and pay" endpoint in one transaction for non-card tenders.
- Do not fire kitchen tickets for a pay-first flow until the payment succeeds, or let the operator choose fire-on-send or fire-on-pay.

#### F-05: Receipt printer is dialed from the cloud to private addresses (SSRF) and cannot reach in-store printers (P1)

**Evidence**

- **Allowed addresses:** `validatePrinterHost` accepts only RFC1918 IPv4 and IPv6 ULA addresses (`server/utils/printerHostValidation.js:1-16, 52-66`), on any port from 1 to 65535 (`restaurantPrinterController.js:46`).
- **The API server opens the connection:** `escpos.sendToPrinter` opens a raw TCP socket from the server and writes bytes to it (`server/services/escposPrinter.js:191-210`).
- **Line breaks pass through:** `encodeText` keeps `\r\n` (`escposPrinter.js:71-73`). Receipts include guest-controlled `special_instructions` and owner-controlled menu item names (`restaurantPrinterController.js:136-156`).
- **Weak gating on printing:** `printTestPage` and `printReceipt` check only membership (no role), and the response reports the connect error (`restaurantPrinterController.js:264-302`).
- **Deployment:** the API runs in Docker/Coolify (`docker-compose.coolify.yml`, with internal Redis at `redis://redis:6379` in `docker-compose.yml:60`).

**User impact**

- **Functional:** a restaurant's 192.168.x.x printer is on the restaurant's network, not the server's, so prints from the cloud never arrive.
- **Security:** anyone can create a restaurant. As its owner they can point "the printer" at internal hosts and ports, probe which are open (open, refused and timeout errors are distinguishable), and send CRLF-separated text lines to internal services. How much damage that does depends on how internal services are authenticated. Treat it as an SSRF finding.

**Fix direction**

- Stop dialing from the server. Use the existing claim-based `restaurant_printer_jobs` queue: a paired in-store device (the POS tablet or a small print bridge) pulls jobs and prints on the local network. Alternatively, support cloud-capable printers (Epson ePOS/Server Direct Print, Star CloudPRNT).
- Until then, disable the server-side ESC/POS dial in production, and require manager permission plus rate limits on test and print.

#### F-06: POS Terminal card payments go to the platform account, not the restaurant (P1, latent)

**Evidence**

- `restaurantPosTerminalService.createPaymentIntent` creates a `card_present` PaymentIntent with no `transfer_data`, `on_behalf_of` or `application_fee_amount`, on the platform key (`server/services/restaurantPosTerminalService.js:20-30`).
- Contrast: online card payments use `transfer_data: { destination }` (`foodController.js:4049`).
- `restaurantPayoutService.js:6-8` states that "Card revenue settles directly to the restaurant's Stripe Connect account (destination charges)." The Terminal path breaks that assumption, and there is no payout route for it.
- `createPosTerminalConnectionToken` has no permission check beyond membership (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:5045-5056`), and the Terminal location comes from restaurant settings, so readers register on the platform account.
- **Why it is latent:** the web client has no Terminal integration (no `card-intents` or `connection-token` calls; the POS says "Use the POS app for card checkout" at `RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:173`). The endpoints are live for native and API clients.

**User impact**

- Once a native POS uses this API, in-store card sales settle to OxSteed. The restaurant is not paid, and OxSteed holds funds it should not.

**Fix direction**

- Create Terminal PaymentIntents as destination charges or direct charges on the restaurant's Connect account (`restaurant_connect_accounts`), and register Terminal locations and readers per store on that account.
- Require `pos.use` for connection tokens.
- Block the route until this is fixed.

#### F-07: Two conflicting sales reports; the web report has wrong day boundaries and revenue rules (P1)

**Evidence**

- **The web uses `/reports/sales`** (`client/src/pages/food/RestaurantReportsPanel.jsx:92`), which:
  - parses ranges as UTC days (`T00:00:00Z`, `restaurantReportsController.js:34-36`) and groups by `DATE(created_at)` in the DB session zone (`:63-66`);
  - counts `SUM(total_cents) WHERE status='completed'` as revenue, including tax, tips and fees, and including unpaid "completed" orders (see F-16) (`:63-80`);
  - picks the most common currency and labels the sum of **all** currencies with it (`:102-112`);
  - computes the average ticket as revenue from completed orders divided by orders from all non-cancelled statuses, which understates it (`:116`).
- **The native app uses `getManagerReports`**, which uses the restaurant time zone and **net collected** payments (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:1644-1697`). The two disagree for the same store.
- **What is missing:** a net-sales/tax/tips/discounts/refunds breakdown, payment-method mix, per-location and per-hour views, labor percentage, and any sales export (CSV exports exist only for credit statements, at `server/routes/food.js:76-78, 203`).

**User impact**

- Owners cannot trust daily sales, cannot reconcile with deposits, and cannot hand an accountant an export.

**Fix direction**

- Write one canonical sales fact model (one row per order and per payment, with location, local business date, gross/discount/net/tax/tip/fees/refund and tender) and build both reports and CSV/XLSX exports on it.
- Define the business day in the store's local time (with a configurable day-start hour).
- Report each currency separately.

#### F-08: Offline resilience is below chain expectations (P1)

**Evidence**

- **Web POS:** failed mutations are "reported (never auto-retried)" (`RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:241-249, 425-431`).
- **Managed screens v2:** offline work becomes an **unpaid draft** that a manager must review and accept after reconnecting. "During a store-wide internet outage, devices … cannot exchange new work with other screens until connectivity returns", and there is "No LAN hub, payment hardware … or automatic financial replay" (`docs/restaurant-screens/implementation.md`).
- **No offline card authorization** (store-and-forward) and no local order relay from POS to KDS.

**User impact**

- An outage at lunch stops payments and stops tickets from reaching the kitchen screen. Staff fall back to paper.

**Fix direction**

- Medium term: an in-store hub. One designated device (or a small appliance) relays orders between POS, KDS and printers over the local network, and syncs to the cloud with the existing revision journal.
- Add offline cash tender with automatic, idempotent replay. The server already has idempotency on payments.
- Evaluate store-and-forward card payments within risk limits (Stripe Terminal offline mode).

### P2

#### F-09: `POST /api/food/restaurants/:id/orders/:orderId/split` always fails (P2)

**Evidence**

- `foodController.splitOrder` inserts columns that do not exist (`customer_user_id`, `payment_mode`, `currency`, `delivery_address`, `special_instructions`) and omits required `business_id` and `queue_number` (`server/controllers/foodController.js:7768-7787`).
- Running the statement against the migrated schema fails with `column "customer_user_id" of relation "food_orders" does not exist`.
- It also sets `total = subtotal`, which drops tax and tip (`:7803-7806`), and has no payment or ticket-state guard.
- The route is live (`server/routes/food.js:231`). The web uses the correct `/api/restaurants/.../pos/orders/:id/split` instead (`FoodMerchantPage.jsx:708`).

**User impact:** any client calling this route gets a 500 error. It is dead code that could be wired up by mistake.

**Fix direction:** remove the route, or alias it to `splitPosOrder`, which has the correct guards.

#### F-10: Hours logic is wrong for overnight schedules, allows one range per day, and a bad time zone crashes ordering (P2)

**Evidence** (all in `server/utils/foodOperatingHours.js`)

- **Overnight hours:** only *today's* schedule is checked (`:72-97`). Reproduced:
  - Tuesday 18:00–02:00 with Wednesday closed: Wednesday 01:00 returns **closed**. Expected open.
  - Monday 18:00–02:00 with Sunday closed: Monday 01:00 returns **open**. Expected closed.
- **One range per day:** one `open`/`close` per day (`:50-58`), so split shifts (lunch and dinner) are impossible.
- **Invalid time zone:** a value like `"Eastern"` makes `Intl.DateTimeFormat` throw `RangeError` (reproduced). Time zones are not validated on write (`foodController.js:5135`, `restaurantAppFlowController.js:1873`), so one bad value makes every order and storefront load fail.
- **Text:** server messages ("Opens at …") are English only.

**Fix direction**

- Evaluate the previous day's overnight spill.
- Support multiple ranges per day, plus holiday and special-date overrides.
- Validate IANA time zones on write and fall back safely on read.
- Return structured hours to the client so it can localize the message.

#### F-11: Dayparting is stored but never enforced; scheduled orders ignore hours and fire immediately (P2)

**Evidence**

- `availability_schedule` is saved on create, update and duplicate (`foodController.js:2532, 5562-5567, 5779`), but no code reads it when quoting or ordering. No client code references it either.
- Scheduled orders only check that the time is in the future and within 48 hours (`foodController.js:3131-3152`). Nothing checks the scheduled time against operating hours.
- The kitchen feed and screens never look at `scheduled_for`, so a 7 PM pre-order shows up on the KDS at noon.

**Fix direction**

- Enforce menu dayparts in `quoteOrder`, `createOrder` and the POS menu.
- Validate scheduled times against hours and the slot capacity for that time.
- Hold scheduled tickets in the kitchen feed until fire time (`scheduled_for − prep time`).

#### F-12: Web onboarding saves empty fulfillment modes; the POS then rejects orders (P2)

**Evidence**

- `createRestaurant` writes `normalizeTextArray(undefined)` = `[]`, which overrides the DB default `{pickup,dine_in,kiosk}` (`foodController.js:2440`). The web form sends no modes (`FoodMerchantPage.jsx:70-83`).
- The online path treats `[]` as `['pickup']` (`foodController.js:3176-3178`).
- The POS path does not, and the POS defaults to `dine_in` (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:3563-3566`; `RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:232`). The modes only get set if the Setup Guide ordering step is completed (`RestaurantSetupGuide.jsx:195-213`).

**User impact:** the first POS sale fails, and online customers only see pickup.

**Fix direction:** use one default (the DB default) everywhere, set the modes at creation, and treat an empty array the same way on every path.

#### F-13: POS table name is silently dropped (P2)

**Evidence:** the web POS sends `table_name` (`RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:388, 445`). The server reads only `tableLabel`/`table_label` (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:3777`).

**User impact:** dine-in tickets reach the kitchen and runners with no table.

**Fix direction:** align the field name and add a contract test.

#### F-14: POS payment modal total excludes tax and service fee (P2)

**Evidence:** `finalTotal = total − discount + tip`, and cash change is computed from it (`RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:119-121`). The server adds tax and service fee (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:3645-3649, 3715`). The payment then records `order.total_cents` (`:413`).

**User impact:** the cashier quotes the wrong amount and gives the wrong change whenever tax is above 0.

**Fix direction:** quote server-side before showing the total (reuse `quoteOrder`), and show tax, fees and the total from the quote.

#### F-15: POS discounts are three hard-coded codes for every restaurant (P2)

**Evidence:** `DEFAULT_POS_DISCOUNTS` contains WELCOME10, LUNCH15 and VIP20 (`server/controllers/restaurantPosController.js:31-35`), with matching `STANDARD_POS_DISCOUNTS` (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:107-111`). Any employee can apply 20% with no reason and no approval (`:242-283`).

**User impact:** owners cannot configure discounts or comps, and there is no control or reporting on discount use.

**Fix direction:** add brand-managed discount and comp definitions with eligibility, maximums, reason codes, manager-approval thresholds and reporting.

#### F-16: Unpaid orders can be marked completed (P2)

**Evidence**

- Only managed v2 screens block completing an unpaid order (`foodController.js:4409`).
- Staff, and PR #612 kitchen-role display tokens, which may set `ready` and `completed` (`foodController.js:4348-4356`), can complete `pay_at_restaurant` orders that are still `unpaid`. The SQL only blocks `pay_now` with `requires_payment` (`:4463-4470`).
- The web sales report counts every completed order as revenue (F-07).

**User impact:** food can leave without a recorded payment, which is a leakage and fraud path, and the reports overstate cash.

**Fix direction:** require `payment_status in (paid, comped)`, or an explicit manager "complete unpaid" action with a reason and audit entry, before completing pickup and dine-in orders on every path.

#### F-17: 86ing is manager-only and applies to the whole brand (P2)

**Evidence:** `setMenuItemAvailability` → `assertManage` (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:8028-8033`), including the `/pos/menu-items/:id/sold-out` route that is meant for POS staff (`server/routes/restaurants.js:183-186`). The availability flag lives on `food_menu_items` (F-02).

**User impact:** a line cook cannot 86 an item, and doing so would 86 it at every store.

**Fix direction:** allow `kitchen.update` or `pos.use` to 86 items per store, with an audit entry and an automatic return-to-stock time.

#### F-18: Network calls are made while the restaurant row is locked (P2)

**Evidence**

- Online `createOrder` locks `food_restaurants ... FOR UPDATE` (`foodController.js:2959-2978`) and then calls Stripe `paymentIntents.retrieve` and `update` inside the same transaction (`:3358, 3576`; the transaction ends at `:3819`).
- `createPosOrder` locks the restaurant row (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:3556-3559`) and then calls the external `calculateGeoRoute` for delivery (`:3688`).
- The lock exists to produce `MAX(queue_number)+1`.

**User impact:** every order for a restaurant (and, in the current model, every store) is serialized behind Stripe or routing latency, which caps throughput at rush hour and causes timeouts.

**Fix direction**

- Allocate queue numbers from a per-store, per-business-day counter row (or a sequence) with a short lock.
- Move Stripe and geo calls outside the transaction: pre-validate before the transaction, then confirm after it.

#### F-19: The tax model is too simple for chains (P2)

**Evidence:** one `tax_rate_bps` per restaurant, applied to the discounted subtotal (`foodController.js:2760-2765`; `restaurantAppFlowController.js:3645-3649`). There are no item tax categories (prepared food, alcohol, grocery), no tax-inclusive (VAT/GST) pricing, no per-location jurisdictions, no tax on delivery or service fees where required, and no tax-exempt orders.

**Fix direction:** add tax categories per item and tax rules per store (inclusive or exclusive, multiple rates, fee taxability), or integrate a tax engine (Avalara, Stripe Tax). Snapshot the applied rules on each order.

#### F-20: The audit trail misses menu, price, tax and settings changes (P2)

**Evidence:** `updateRestaurant` writes `business_audit_log` only for status changes (`foodController.js:5427-5450`). Menu item, price, modifier, tax-rate, service-fee and discount changes are not audit-logged. (POS receipts, kitchen, inventory and platform-admin access are audited.)

**Fix direction:** add an append-only change log (who, when, before and after) for menu and pricing, tax and fees, hours, permissions, devices and printers, with an owner-visible history.

#### F-21: Shared-terminal staff identity is unused and weak (P2)

**Evidence**

- `setStaffPin` and `validateStaffPin` exist (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:5538-5619`), but no client calls them, so a shared POS needs each person to log in to their OxSteed account.
- PINs are not unique per restaurant (two staff can share one, and the first match wins), there is no lockout or rate limit, and validation runs bcrypt against every active PIN in turn (slow for large crews).
- Label-only PINs (`staff_user_id NULL`) bypass the upsert key and duplicate.

**Fix direction:** add a PIN "clock-in and switch user" flow on paired POS devices, with a unique PIN per store, per-device rate limiting and lockout, and fast lookup (a keyed hash plus bcrypt).

#### F-22: Workforce management is missing: time clock, scheduling by person, labor cost, tip pooling (P2)

**Evidence:** `restaurant_crew_shifts` has no `staff_user_id` (they are operating shifts with a label: `337_restaurant_crew_shifts.sql`). POS shifts are cash drawers. There are no clock-in/out records, wages, breaks, overtime, labor-percentage reporting, tip pooling or tip-out.

**Fix direction:** add a time clock (PIN or device), schedules by employee, wage rates, labor in reports, and tip distribution rules. Alternatively, integrate 7shifts or Homebase.

#### F-23: Inventory lacks per-store stock, unit conversion and food cost (P2)

**Evidence**

- Inventory quantity is per restaurant only.
- Transfers are between storage areas (`restaurantInventoryOperationsController.js:503-509`).
- Menu-to-ingredient mappings have `required_quantity` with no unit conversion or yield.
- Costing is limited to estimated waste cost (`:218, 245`). There is no theoretical vs actual usage, COGS, food-cost percentage, vendor price history or PO-to-invoice matching.

**Fix direction:** add per-store inventory, recipes with units and yields, counts by period, COGS and variance reports, and vendor catalogs.

#### F-24: i18n gaps in the restaurant UI and server-generated text (P2)

**Evidence**

- `RestaurantCreditReservePanel.jsx` and `RestaurantCreditExposurePanel.jsx` do not use `useTranslation` at all and render English literals (e.g. "Buy credit reserve", "Credit exposure", "Outstanding exposure").
- `RestaurantCreditManager.jsx:219-372` hard-codes headings and buttons ("Restaurant credit", "Grant credit directly", "Approve credit").
- The POS placeholder "Paste the OxSteed Credit hold ID" is hard-coded (`RestaurantPOSPage.jsx:185`).
- Server-generated customer text is English only: hours messages (`foodOperatingHours.js:81-110`), and receipt labels and money formatting fixed to `en-US` (`restaurantPrinterController.js:70-80, 162-185`).
- The locale catalogs themselves are complete for en, es, fr, ht and ar.

**Fix direction:** move these strings into the `food.*` and `restaurants.*` keys in all 5 locales. Return codes and parameters from the server and localize on the client. Print receipts in the restaurant's chosen locale and currency format.

#### F-25: RTL layout uses physical CSS properties (P2)

**Evidence:** `client/src/styles/FoodPages.css` has 42 physical `left`/`right`/`margin-left`/`text-align:right` declarations, 7 logical ones and no `[dir=rtl]` overrides. `RestaurantCreditPage.css` has 8 physical ones. The screens CSS is already logical.

**User impact:** Arabic POS, KDS and merchant pages show misaligned prices, badges and close buttons.

**Fix direction:** convert to logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `inset-inline-end`, `text-align: end`) and add an RTL visual check to the existing browser checks.

#### F-26: Surface and device sprawl (P2)

**Evidence**

- **Three device and pairing systems:**
  - `restaurant_app_devices` with pairing codes and device tokens (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:2712-2830`);
  - `display_pairings` from PR #612 (`911_display_pairings.sql`, `restaurantDisplayController.js`);
  - managed screens v2 (`887_restaurant_screen_sync.sql`, `restaurantScreensController.js`).
- **Two printing models:** server-side ESC/POS (F-05) and the claim-based `restaurant_printer_jobs` queue.
- **Duplicate endpoints:**
  - location create/update in both `foodController.js:7165-7275` and `restaurantAppFlowController.js:2220-2390`;
  - split in two places (F-09);
  - sales reports in two places (F-07).

**User impact:** owners must learn which pairing flow applies where, fixes land in one copy but not the other, and permission rules drift.

**Fix direction:** converge on one device identity (role, store and capabilities), one print path (the job queue plus an in-store agent), and one controller per resource. Deprecate the rest behind adapters.

#### F-27: Display fleet (PR #612) is owner-only; pairing tokens never expire (P2)

**Evidence**

- Claim, list, refresh and unpair all require `effective_role === 'owner'` (`restaurantDisplayController.js:166-174, 450`), so store managers cannot replace a broken kitchen TV.
- `verifyDisplayPairingToken` has no expiry or rotation (`:144-162`), unlike app devices (`786_restaurant_device_token_expiry.sql`).

**Fix direction:** allow `display.manage` (manager), and later scope it per store. Add token expiry and rotation on heartbeat, and a "last seen and revoke" view.

### P3

#### F-28: Location primary flip is not transactional; weak validation (P3)

**Evidence:** `UPDATE ... is_primary = FALSE` runs before the `INSERT`/`UPDATE`, outside any transaction (`foodController.js:7175-7203, 7225-7267`). An invalid `status` hits the CHECK constraint and returns a 500 after primary has already been cleared. Via the API, `latitude: null` becomes `0` (`Number(null)`).

**Fix direction:** wrap the change in a transaction, validate the enums, and treat null or empty coordinates as null.

#### F-29: The locations UI is create-only (P3)

**Evidence:** `FoodLocationsPanel.jsx` only creates and lists locations. There is no edit, pause or close, and no time-zone, hours or phone field, so new locations get `timezone='UTC'`.

**Fix direction:** add full location management (this becomes part of F-02).

#### F-30: The PR #612 display hook test cannot run, and `src/**` tests are not in CI (P3)

**Evidence**

- `node --test client/src/hooks/useDisplayPoller.test.mjs` fails with `ERR_MODULE_NOT_FOUND: .../src/utils/displayBackoff` (an extensionless ESM import).
- CI runs only `client/scripts/*.test.mjs` (`.github/workflows/ci.yml:76`), so `src/hooks/*.test.mjs` and `src/utils/display*.test.mjs` never run in CI (the utility tests do pass locally).

**Fix direction:** fix the import resolution (add extensions or use a loader) and add `src/**/*.test.mjs` to the CI glob.

#### F-31: Some ticket actions check membership only (P3)

**Evidence:** POS hold and reopen (`updatePosTicket` non-void path, `restaurantAppFlowController.js:3855-3883`), `capturePosCardPayment` (`:5193-5203`), and printing (F-05) need no specific permission. Any business member, including representative and support-agent roles, can hold or reopen tickets.

**Fix direction:** require `pos.use` (or `orders.manage`) for these.

#### F-32: Queue numbers are per restaurant, not per store (P3)

**Evidence:** `MAX(queue_number)+1` per restaurant per day (`restaurantAppFlowController.js:3716-3723`).

**User impact:** two stores share one number sequence, which confuses pickup boards.

**Fix direction:** keep a per-store daily counter (see F-18).

#### F-33: No order pacing or busy mode (P3)

**Evidence:** no throttling or capacity logic in the online order path (grep for pacing/throttle/capacity finds nothing). There is only a manual pause and `temporarily_closed_until` (`foodController.js:3031-3043`).

**Fix direction:** add orders-per-slot limits, automatic prep-time extension under load, and a one-tap "busy" mode for each channel.

## 5. Flow review: owner, staff and guest

### 5.1 Owner onboarding (web)

**Current flow**

1. Create a restaurant (name, address, currency).
2. Setup Guide: profile, location, hours, menu, publish.
3. Settings: tax rate, payments, locations, promotions, printer.
4. Operations: devices, checklists, inventory and the other modules.

**Friction and risk**

- No time zone is captured, so hours and queue numbers run in UTC (F-01).
- `fulfillment_modes` is saved as `[]`, so the POS fails until a later step (F-12).
- Location creation has no time zone or hours fields, and no edit (F-29).
- The printer setup suggests a LAN IP that the cloud cannot reach (F-05).
- Three device-pairing flows to learn (F-26).

**Suggested flow**

1. One wizard: brand profile, then first store (address, **auto-detected time zone**, hours, tax), then ordering channels (pre-checked defaults), then menu import (CSV, photo-to-menu, or copy from a template).
2. "Set up your store hardware": a single pairing code for any device, where the device picks its role (POS, KDS, kiosk, queue board, print bridge).
3. Test order end-to-end (online → KDS → ready → pickup) before going live.

### 5.2 Counter staff (web POS)

**Current flow**

1. Open a POS shift.
2. Build the ticket. The table name is lost (F-13).
3. Pay, which only managers can do (F-03). The modal total omits tax (F-14).
4. Two requests run; if the second fails, the result is a duplicate order (F-04).
5. Cash change is computed on the wrong total.

**Suggested flow**

1. PIN sign-in on a shared device (F-21), which opens or joins that person's drawer.
2. Build the ticket with live server quoting (tax, fees, discounts).
3. Choose "Send" (fire to kitchen) or "Pay" (one idempotent create-and-pay call). The cashier can tender cash, card or mobile. Discounts above a threshold, comps, refunds and voids prompt for a manager PIN.
4. The receipt prints through the in-store print path, and change due is shown correctly.

### 5.3 Kitchen (KDS)

**Current:** station routing, item-level progress, and quality check then ready are good.

**Gaps**

- Scheduled orders appear immediately (F-11).
- Kitchen displays can complete unpaid orders (F-16).
- 86ing needs a manager (F-17).

**Suggested**

- Hold scheduled tickets until fire time.
- Show "unpaid" prominently. Let expo mark ready, and let only the counter (after payment) mark picked up.
- Add a one-tap 86 on KDS and POS with an automatic return-to-stock time, scoped per store.

### 5.4 Guest (online)

**Current flow**

1. Browse.
2. Quote.
3. Order with an idempotency key.
4. Pay now, at the counter or with credit.
5. Track with a token.

**Gaps**

- The open/closed state is wrong outside UTC (F-01) and for overnight hours (F-10).
- Dayparted items are always orderable (F-11).
- There is no store picker for multi-location brands (F-02).
- There is no pacing, so the kitchen can be overwhelmed (F-33).

**Suggested**

- Pick the nearest open store first (that store's menu, prices, hours and wait time).
- Offer schedule-ahead slots limited by capacity.
- Send clear "closed, order for later" messages in the guest's language.

### 5.5 Manager and HQ reporting

**Current:** the web report uses UTC days and gross "completed" totals, while the native report uses net collected (F-07). There is no export and no comparison across stores.

**Suggested**

- One sales fact model. Daily close (Z-report) per store and drawer.
- Exports (CSV/XLSX, plus an accounting journal).
- An HQ roll-up with store comparison and labor and food-cost percentages once F-22 and F-23 land.

## 6. Prioritized roadmap to franchise grade

### Now: 0–4 weeks (stop the bleeding; single-store correctness)

1. **F-01** Time zone: capture at onboarding, copy from the business, add a settings field, backfill, validate (plus the time-zone parts of **F-10**).
2. **F-05** Disable server-side ESC/POS dialing in production. Route receipts through the printer-job queue to a paired in-store device.
3. **F-04** POS checkout idempotency: a stable `clientOrderId`, replay in `createPosOrder`, and retrying Pay reuses the order.
4. **F-03** Let `pos.use` staff tender payments in their own drawer, with manager approval only for refunds, voids and large discounts. Start a single permission service.
5. **F-06** Block or route POS Terminal intents to the restaurant's Connect account, and gate connection tokens.
6. **F-16** Block completing unpaid orders without a manager reason.
7. **F-12, F-13, F-14** Fix the POS fulfillment default, the table field and the server-quoted totals.
8. **F-09** Remove the broken food split route.
9. **F-07 (part)** Switch the web report to the restaurant time zone and to net collected revenue, report each currency separately, and add a CSV export.

### Next: 1–3 months (operator-grade core)

1. **F-02 phase 1:** a first-class store (`location_id` on orders, payments, shifts, devices, printers, inventory), store-scoped roles, and per-store hours, time zone and tax.
2. A brand menu with per-store overrides (price, availability/86, tax category), and dayparting enforcement (**F-11**).
3. A canonical sales fact model, daily close (Z-report) and exports (**F-07**).
4. Shared-device PIN switching (**F-21**) and a time clock (**F-22**, basic).
5. Configurable discounts and comps with approvals (**F-15**), and audit logs for menu, price and settings (**F-20**).
6. Hours v2: multiple ranges, overnight, holidays (**F-10**), and order pacing (**F-33**).
7. Converge devices and printing (**F-26**, **F-27**).
8. i18n and RTL cleanup (**F-24**, **F-25**), and CI for `src/**` tests (**F-30**).

### Later: 3–9 months (chain and franchise scale)

1. An HQ console: brand → region → store hierarchy, menu publishing, store comparisons, compliance (checklist, audit and training) roll-ups, and franchisee vs HQ data boundaries (the royalty reporting hook already exists in `franchiseRoyaltyService`).
2. Offline v2: an in-store hub, offline cash with auto-replay, and Terminal store-and-forward within risk limits (**F-08**).
3. Tax engine integration and tax-inclusive (VAT) markets (**F-19**).
4. Inventory v2: recipes with units and yields, per-store counts, COGS and variance (**F-23**).
5. Labor scheduling, tip pooling and payroll export (**F-22**).
6. Reservations, tables and floor plan, waitlist with SMS, coursing and seat-level splits.
7. Integrations: delivery marketplaces (order injection and menu sync), accounting (QuickBooks/Xero journal), BI export.
8. Scale work: per-store queue counters and no network calls inside row locks (**F-18**), plus load tests at peak volume for 50+ stores.
