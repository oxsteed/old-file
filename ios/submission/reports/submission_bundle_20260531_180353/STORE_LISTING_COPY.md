## Description
OxSteed helps people find and manage real-world local services in one place.

Use OxSteed to post jobs, compare offers, connect with helpers, message securely, and track service progress. If you provide services, you can discover work opportunities, manage requests, and coordinate with customers.

What you can do in OxSteed:
- Post service requests with clear scope and location details
- Browse local helpers and compare bids
- Message customers and helpers in-app
- Report or block users from messaging when needed
- Manage recurring service bookings
- Track payments for real-world services
- Explore learning guides and practical workflows across roles

OxSteed supports multiple user roles, including customers, helpers, business owners, consultants, tool owners, and investors.

Privacy and control:
- In-app Privacy Policy access
- Data export options
- In-app account deletion flow

Payments note:
OxSteed supports payments for real-world services performed outside the app.
