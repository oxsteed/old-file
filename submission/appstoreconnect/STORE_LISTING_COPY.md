## Description
OxSteed brings local service work, home task planning, and practical learning into one app.

Use OxSteed to post service requests, browse jobs and helpers, compare bids, message in app, and keep work organized from request to completion. The app also includes Life, Growth, and Learn sections for home-care tasks, goals, saved helpers, growth opportunities, and step-by-step guides.

What you can do:
- Post jobs with category, budget, location, urgency, and optional photos
- Browse local jobs, helpers, and map results
- Place and review bids, quotes, bookings, and job updates
- Message customers or helpers in app
- Manage recurring service needs
- Track personal goals, home-care tasks, and saved helpers
- Explore practical guides for customers, helpers, business owners, consultants, tool owners, and investors
- Manage notification preferences and account settings

OxSteed supports payment-related workflows for real-world services performed outside the app.
