# Internal Legal Framework Development Plan
## Reducing External Counsel Costs through Strategic Internal Work

**Date:** April 30, 2026  
**Prepared By:** Senior Counsel (Internal Legal Team)  
**For:** CEO – OxSteed  
**Goal:** Reduce external counsel spend from $50-90K to $15-25K by completing 70% of work internally

---

## Executive Summary

**Situation:** OxSteed needs legal framework before launch. Industry precedent shows marketplaces (TaskRabbit, Nextdoor, Andi, OfferUp, Roadie) have established legal structures.

**Approach:** As internal senior counsel, I will research industry-standard documents and draft 90%-complete frameworks for:
- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Data Processing Agreements
- Helper/Seller Agreements
- Dispute Resolution Process
- Background Check Procedures
- Payment/Escrow Procedures
- Incident Response/Breach Notification Procedures

**Outcome:** External counsel reviews/polishes our work (4-6 weeks of drafting becomes 2 weeks of review).

**Cost Impact:**
- **Without this approach:** $50-90K external counsel (150-280 billable hours at $200-300/hr)
- **With this approach:** ~$15-25K external counsel (50-80 billable hours for QA/compliance review only)
- **Savings:** $25-65K (35-65% reduction)

**Internal Cost:** ~80-120 hours senior counsel time (you already employ me; minimal marginal cost)

## Quality Assurance: How This Stays Safe

**Question:** How do we ensure internal drafts are legal-quality without external counsel?

**Answer:** Industry standards + established patterns:

1. **I'm researching PROVEN frameworks** – TaskRabbit, Nextdoor, Andi, OfferUp, Roadie have all gone through legal reviews. Their public documents (ToS, Privacy Policies) are battle-tested in court.

2. **GDPR/CCPA have established patterns** – There's not much variation in compliant privacy policies. Compliance language is standardized across industries.

3. **I'm doing 90% drafts, not 100%** – External counsel gets 90% complete documents that just need:
   - Jurisdiction-specific tweaks
   - Risk assessment (their job)
   - Business-specific customization
   - Final compliance audit

4. **External counsel does the risky parts** – Employment law (AB5 risk), risk assessment, and compliance sign-off stay with external counsel. I'm handling the "commodity" legal work.

5. **Internal review before sending to counsel** – I'll review all drafts for internal consistency, industry alignment, and obvious gaps before external counsel sees them.
