### Group 2: Household delegate (items 51-100) — built 2026-11-19

- **D-34. Household delegate model: the household is the owner's account plus a delegates list; there is no separate household entity.** A delegate is a person the owner invites (by email, resolved to an account when one matches) who, after accepting, may act only within the scope the owner grants (`book`, `schedule`, `prepare`, `coordinate`, `view_records`, `manage_addresses`, `manage_tasks`, `view_calendar`). A delegate never pays, never changes the owner's account, never sees payment internals (booking reads for delegates carry no amounts, no deposit state, no payout data), and never acts outside scope (every household endpoint re-reads the live delegation row, so revocation closes access immediately). The model reuses the G-6/G-7 token/consent machinery in shape (invite token, accept/decline, expiry, revocation, activity log) but is a separate grant: a recipient is who the work is for on one booking; a delegate is who may act for the owner across bookings. A delegate who books on the owner's behalf creates the booking with `customer_id` = owner (the deposit payer) and `booked_by_user_id` = delegate, so the ownership label (item 54) always shows both. Flip: introduce a first-class household entity (shared billing, multi-owner) only by an explicit call; that is a different product.
- **D-35. Delegation lifetime: 90-day default, renewable, reminded.** A new delegation expires 90 days after the invite (not 7: a booking link is a one-off, a delegation is an ongoing role; 90 days is the recorded call). The owner and the delegate both see days-to-expiry on every delegation read; either side can trigger a renewal reminder, and the owner renews consent (item 83) for a fresh 90 days from the renewal date. Expiry is enforced live (expired rows grant nothing) and surfaced, never silent. Flip: change the 90-day TTL (one constant, `DELEGATION_TTL_MS`) or add auto-renewal.
- **D-36. Spending approval is a request the owner decides; a delegate never self-approves and no money moves.** A delegate with `book` scope files a spending approval request (amount, reason, booking); only the owner approves or denies it; a decided request cannot be re-decided. Approval is a record only: it never charges, never moves Credit, never changes a price. Flip: wire approved requests into a payment flow only by an explicit call.
- **D-37. Private notes stay private; shared data is explicit.** Saved locations carry `private_notes` (owner-only, never returned to a delegate) separate from `entry_instructions` (shared with the delegate and the provider). Entry instruction presets are a fixed list (`gate_code`, `parking`, `pets`, `stairs`, `key_location`, `none`) stored on the location, not free text the provider must parse. Flip: add presets, or let the owner share a private note per location.
- **D-38. Calendar-only sharing is a scope, not a separate product.** A delegation whose only scope is `view_calendar` reads the shared appointment calendar and nothing else: no bookings, no addresses, no documents, no tasks. The scope preview (item 81) lists exactly what each scope grants before the delegate accepts, and the delegate accepts the scope as shown. Flip: add a separate calendar-share link (token) instead of the scope.
- **D-39. Closure is a receipt, and a returning delegate is re-invited, never silently restored.** Revocation and expiry are immediate and total; closing a delegation writes a closure receipt (who closed it, when, why, what it covered, upcoming visits reassigned to whom) that both sides can read afterwards. A past delegate is brought back by a fresh invitation with a fresh token and fresh consent, never by flipping the old row back to accepted. Flip: allow reinstatement of a closed row (would erase the audit trail the receipt exists to preserve).

### Groups 5-6: Business owner (201-250) and Business team member (251-300)

Built on PR #809 (Emile 2026-11-23: one branch while #809 is open; no questions, industry-standard defaults, every choice recorded here for review). Tracker: `docs/persona-roadmap/group5-6.md`. UI lives in two new Business Hub tabs: **Owner console** (owner/manager) and **My work** (any active member); the server re-checks every call.

- **D-194. Listing claims link a directory listing only (item 201).** Pattern: Google Business Profile / Yelp claim. The owner of an OxSteed business submits an evidence-backed claim (relationship + evidence text) for an unclaimed `prospect_businesses` listing; OxSteed admins (`manage_operations`) approve or reject; the claimant can never review their own claim. Approval only sets `prospect_businesses.matched_business_id` and auto-rejects other pending claims on that listing. It grants no capability, money access, or profile change. A contact match (email domain or phone matching the listing) is recorded as a review signal, not an auto-approval. Search never returns listing contact details. Licence gate: only listings licensed for `marketing_outreach` are searchable/claimable, since owner contact is outreach under the import licence. Flip: auto-approve on contact match; email-code verification via Resend.
- **D-195. Item 202 (ownership application) already exists** as the `business.own` access request (progressive access) with linked launch intake; not rebuilt.
- **D-196. Launch status and checklists are computed, not stored (203-205).** Status is `hidden` (admin-hidden) > `live` (listed and published) > `ready_to_publish` (all required verification checks pass and at least one service is ready) > `in_progress`. Required verification: name, 40+ char description, phone, city/state, service area, hours, payments and payouts enabled. Recommended (optional): logo, cover photo, EIN, verified badge. A service is ready when active, not paused, 20+ char description, a price, a duration, and (if online booking is on) at least one available staff member. Manager+ with `business.view` can read it. Nothing publishes automatically. Flip: required/optional per check.
- **D-197. Regional offering rules are per service: everywhere / only listed / everywhere except (207).** Areas are US state codes and ZIP prefixes. Enforced on customer bookings using the booking's `service_state`/`service_postal_code` or the customer's profile state/ZIP; under a restriction with no known location the booking returns 422 `service_area_required`, outside the area 422 `outside_service_area`. Changes are versioned (`offering_version`, 409 `offering_changed`) and logged in `business_service_offering_history`.
- **D-198. Service pause blocks customer bookings and hides the service publicly (210).** Pattern: Square "unavailable for online booking". A pause needs a private reason and optional auto-resume time; past `paused_until` the service is bookable again without a write. Staff-created walk-ins are not blocked (owners can still book a known customer). Existing appointments are untouched. Pause/resume are logged.
- **D-199. Open assignments and the heatmap are read models (217, 218).** Open = upcoming pending/confirmed/in-progress visits with no assignee or an assignee who is no longer an active member; each row lists staff eligible for that service, and assignment reuses the existing `PUT /appointments/:id/staff` (which now also writes a structured `business_appointment_assignment_events` row). The heatmap counts non-cancelled bookings by weekday x hour in the business time zone (next or last 1-26 weeks) against staffed hours from weekly availability; hours with more bookings than staff are outlined. Gates: `jobs.assign` / `calendar.view_all`, manager+.
- **D-200. Handoff timeline and repeat records come from authoritative rows (220, 222).** The timeline unions assignment events, member joins and role changes, offboarding records and approved shift swaps (later slices add ownership transfers, deputies and shift handovers). Repeat customers are those with 2+ completed visits (by customer id, else email), with visits, first/last visit, typical gap, services and lifetime value; gated by `customers.view_all`.
- **D-201. Service issues: customer-reported, 30-day window, owner-resolved (225).** Pattern: Housecall Pro / Jobber callbacks. Only the visit's customer can report (categories quality, incomplete, damage, late/no-show, conduct, billing, other), on a completed, in-progress or no-show visit, within 30 days, one open report per visit, idempotent by request id. The owner is notified; manager+ with `bookings.manage` moves it to in review, resolved or closed (a customer-visible response is required to close); the customer is notified and sees the response under My bookings. No refund or rating change happens automatically. Flip: window length; auto-open a support ticket.
- **D-202. Approval thresholds cover quotes, expenses and refund requests (226).** Pattern: Jobber/Housecall Pro approval limits. Owner-set dollar limits (empty = no limit). Quotes above the limit can be sent only by the owner or a manager holding `quotes.approve_discounts`, or after one of them approves that exact total (editing the total voids the approval). Expenses above the limit recorded by anyone other than the owner or a `payouts.manage` holder are stored `pending`, and an approver who is not the requester approves or rejects them. Refund requests above the limit (or full refunds) must come from the owner; they still queue for OxSteed admin approval as before (G-20), so no refund ever executes from this path.
- **D-203. Work authorization policy gates the start of work (227).** Options: no extra step / booking must be confirmed / customer must have accepted a quote linked to the visit; plus the minimum role allowed to start work (employee by default). Enforced when an appointment moves to `in_progress`. Staff-created walk-ins also bypass region and pause rules (D-197/D-198) but not this policy.
- **D-204. Cancellation settings are business defaults (228).** Free-cancellation window, customer reschedule allowed, auto no-show hours (stored in the existing `business_booking_rules`), a default late-cancel fee % used when a service has no fee of its own (a service fee of 0 means "use the business default"), and a no-show fee % recorded on the visit when it is marked no-show (same `cancellation_fee_cents` column and collection path as the late-cancel fee; nothing is charged automatically). Owner-only, versioned with a snapshot history.
- **D-205. Invoice overview is a standard A/R aging view (231).** Pattern: QuickBooks / Jobber aging report. Open = any invoice not draft, paid or cancelled; balance = total − amount paid. Buckets: not yet due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days past due date; plus invoiced and collected this month, draft count and the 20 oldest overdue invoices. Visible to anyone holding a finance read code (`invoices.view` / `payments.view` / `payouts.view`).
- **D-206. Refund tracker lists the business's queued refund requests (232).** Reads the same `money_movement_approvals` rows (`job_refund`, payload businessId) the per-job list uses, business-wide, newest 200, with status counts (waiting, approved, declined, refunded, failed). Read-only; refunds still execute only through OxSteed admin approval (G-20).
- **D-207. Payout reconciliation matches card payments to owner-recorded bank deposits (233).** Pattern: Square / QuickBooks bank-deposit matching, with no bank feed (no paid vendor). Card-collected items are paid invoices and paid bookings that carry a Stripe payment intent; cash/checks are excluded. An owner or `payouts.manage` holder records a deposit (amount, date, bank reference, note) and ticks the payments it covers; each payment can be matched to only one deposit, only this business's payments are accepted, and the difference (usually processing fees) is shown per deposit. Deleting a deposit frees its payments. Nothing moves money. Flip: automatic Stripe payout import.
- **D-208. Business expense categories roll up to the fixed base set (234).** Pattern: QuickBooks sub-accounts / Expensify categories. A business defines up to 100 named categories (unique per business, case-insensitive), each mapped to one of the eight standard categories used for reports and taxes. Choosing one on an expense sets the base category on the server; remapping a category updates its expenses' base category; categories are archived, not deleted. Managed by `invoices.create` holders (the existing expense-write code).
