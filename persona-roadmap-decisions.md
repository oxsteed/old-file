## Open decisions (Phase 1 input — build proceeds on the default below unless Emile says otherwise)

- **D-10. Household delegate / recipient / sponsor model (G-6/G-7).** DECIDED and built 2026-11-19 (G-6/G-7 track, commit "G-6/G-7: book for another person + site-contact role"). The book-for-another primitive (recipient columns, recipient view/cancel, token status page) was already live; this track closed the delivery, acceptance, expiry, revocation, site-contact, and jobs legs.
  - **Recipient account vs token:** both. The booker attaches an existing account by user id (API) or by email (resolved server-side, case-insensitive, active accounts only; a non-matching email silently stays named-only, and the booker sees `recipientUserId: null`). Or the booker names a recipient (name/phone) who is token-linked and needs no account. A signed-in recipient who accepts via the token link has their account linked on accept (decline never links).
  - **Who sees what:** the recipient reads their booking (status, schedule, site contact) and can cancel per the existing cancel rules; the site contact gets a scoped read (status, schedule, address, helper reference) and is admitted to that booking's chat thread only. Neither sees payment internals (the booking read shape carries none; the site-contact shape additionally strips recipient details and both links), and neither can change price, payout, or the booker's account. A random other user gets 403 everywhere. The recipient is NOT admitted to chat (read + cancel only); the site contact cannot cancel, accept/decline, or touch agreements, payments, or booking mutations (every writable chat caller layers a customer/provider role check on thread membership, and the site contact passes none of them).
  - **Liability and payment instrument:** the sponsor (booker) pays via their own separate payment instrument; the deposit is charged to `booking.customer_id` (the booker) and settles/refunds against the booker only. The recipient never becomes the payer. Recipient-pays-through-their-own-instrument is DEFERRED (out of scope for this gate): it needs a recipient payment-instrument flow and a liability decision, not just a flag flip.
  - **Status-link delivery:** through the channel booking confirmations already use: `sendNotification` (in-app notification plus push/email per the recipient's prefs) with a realtime socket event. Account recipients and account site contacts get the link that way (new notification types `booking_recipient_invite`, `booking_site_contact_link`, `booking_recipient_response`, with localized templates in `server/utils/i18n.js` and entries in `server/utils/notificationTypeCatalog.js` so each type has a per-type opt-out toggle in Settings; the N3 sync test in `notificationsPrefsToggles.db.test.js` fails the suite if a new send type is not cataloged). Named-only parties have no account to notify, so the booker shares the link from the booking card (existing copy-link UI, now also showing the recipient invite link). No new channel was invented. (Found while building: new notification types with no template title insert NULL into `notifications.title` and fail silently; the three templates above are the fix.)
  - **Token expiry:** 7 days from creation, shared by both links. No product precedent exists (quote lookup codes are 15-minute one-time challenges; invoice public tokens never expire), so 7 days is the recorded call. Expired and revoked links answer exactly like unknown ones (404).
  - **Acceptance and consent:** the recipient accepts or declines via their own invite token (or from their account when linked). Acceptance records `recipient_consent_status`, `recipient_consented_at`, and method (`token` or `account`); a second response is 409. The booker is notified of the response.
  - **Revocation:** the booker can revoke before acceptance; revocation is booking-level and kills both links at once (separate per-link revocation deferred as unnecessary complexity). After acceptance the booking stands and revocation is refused (400).
  - **Two tokens, not one:** the recipient invite token and the site-contact status token are distinct, so a site contact can never accept or decline on the recipient's behalf. Only the booker ever sees the recipient token (create response and booker reads); reads for anyone else strip it.
  - **Jobs carry-over:** an expired booking auto-posted as a job carries recipient and site-contact columns onto the job; both appear in the recipient's/site contact's `GET /jobs/me/list` and single-job read as a `linked` viewer (address yes, escrow/private notes/money no; budget stays because the public feed already shows it). The booker's private notes are nulled for linked viewers in the list.
  - Flip: shorten/lengthen the 7-day TTL (one constant, `STATUS_LINK_TTL_MS`); split revocation per link; admit the recipient to chat; build recipient-pays.
