## Key Insight: Why This Legal Research is Valid

**The sources I'm using are:**
1. **Published legal documents** (public ToS, Privacy Policies, Terms) – These are the result of extensive legal review by leading platforms
2. **Regulatory guidance** (GDPR.eu, CCPA, DOL, FTC) – Official government guidance on compliance
3. **Established legal standards** (FLSA economic realities, FCRA procedures) – Settled law that doesn't change frequently
4. **Vendor documentation** (Stripe, Checkr, AWS) – Best practices from service providers

**What I'm NOT doing:**
- Inventing new legal structures (using proven frameworks)
- Bypassing regulatory requirements (following official guidance)
- Skipping risky areas (employment law, GDPR/CCPA go to external counsel)
- Creating final legal documents without review (drafts go to counsel for review)

**This is standard practice in large companies:**
- Senior internal counsel researches and drafts frameworks
- External counsel reviews for compliance and jurisdiction-specific issues
- Cost savings come from reducing billable research/drafting hours
