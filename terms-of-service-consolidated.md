## 7. OxSteed Pay - Escrow, Fund Holding, and Release

### 7.1 Mutual Opt-In

OxSteed Pay is **never automatic.** It activates only when both the Customer and the Helper independently and affirmatively elect to use it for a specific, identified job. Neither party may unilaterally activate it on behalf of the other. The election is job-specific and does not apply to future jobs between the same parties.

### 7.2 Fund Holding Mechanism

OxSteed Pay uses Stripe Connect to hold payment funds in escrow during a job. When a Customer submits payment, the funds are authorized on the Customer's payment method and held in Stripe's secure, segregated system. **Funds are held BY Stripe, not by OxSteed.** OxSteed does not have direct access to or control of these funds; OxSteed facilitates the holding only. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant and maintains industry-standard security for payment processing.

### 7.3 Fund Ownership

Until the job is complete and funds are released, the money belongs to the Customer who made the payment. The Helper has no claim to the funds until release conditions are met. OxSteed does not own, control, or have access to escrowed funds and remains a neutral third party. Held funds are not OxSteed's assets and are not commingled with OxSteed's operating funds.

> **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:** Escrowed funds are NOT insured by the FDIC or any government agency. Stripe maintains commercial security standards to prevent loss or theft, but no payment infrastructure is risk-free.

### 7.4 Authorization and Consent

By posting a job and proceeding to payment, you authorize the escrow process. You will receive a confirmation showing the authorization amount, job details, and timeline for fund release. You may cancel the payment before the job begins, subject to Section 7.6.

### 7.5 Release Triggers

Held funds will be released to the Helper when:

- Both the Customer **and** the Helper confirm job completion through the platform; or
- The Customer fails to respond to a completion confirmation request within 48 hours of the Helper marking the job complete (funds auto-release after this window); or
- OxSteed issues a resolution decision in the Helper's favor following the dispute process in Section 10.

Funds typically arrive in the Helper's connected bank account within 5-7 business days of release, subject to Stripe's payout schedule.

### 7.6 Refund Triggers

Held funds will be refunded to the Customer when:

- Both parties mutually agree to cancel the job before work begins;
- The Helper fails to appear or abandons the job, as documented by the Customer;
- The job is cancelled by either party at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time; or
- OxSteed issues a resolution decision in the Customer's favor following the dispute process in Section 10.

Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days of approval. Processing times vary by bank and payment method.

### 7.7 No Interest on Held Funds

OxSteed does not pay interest on held funds to either party. Any interest or float accruing on escrow balances due to Stripe's payment processing timelines is retained by OxSteed as an administrative fee.

### 7.8 Stripe Connected Account Agreement

Helpers must connect a Stripe Express account as a condition of eligibility to receive payment. By connecting a Stripe Express account, you agree to Stripe's [Connected Account Agreement](https://stripe.com/legal/connect-account), which includes Stripe's own terms, privacy policy, and compliance obligations. OxSteed is not a party to your agreement with Stripe and is not responsible for Stripe's actions, errors, or service interruptions.

### 7.9 Service Interruptions and Recovery

If Stripe experiences downtime or service disruptions resulting in delays to fund release, OxSteed is not liable. If Stripe's systems are compromised or funds are lost due to security breach, OxSteed is not liable; your recourse is through Stripe and Stripe's insurance. OxSteed will assist in investigating and reporting any issues to Stripe but cannot recover lost or misappropriated funds directly.

### 7.10 Chargebacks Bypass Escrow

Initiating a credit-card chargeback with your card issuer bypasses OxSteed Pay's protection and the dispute process in Section 10. Chargebacks incur a $25 administrative fee per transaction (charged to the disputed amount or your payment method on file). Chargebacks filed in bad faith may result in immediate account termination. We will contest any chargeback we believe is invalid. Winning a chargeback does not exempt you from OxSteed's dispute decision.

### 7.11 Legal Holds

If OxSteed receives a legal order, subpoena, or court order regarding funds in escrow, those funds may be frozen and held pending legal resolution. OxSteed will notify the affected user where permitted by law.

### 7.12 Platform Continuity

If OxSteed ceases operations, is acquired, or undergoes a change of control, OxSteed will make commercially reasonable efforts to release held funds to the appropriate party, transfer escrow obligations to a successor entity, or refund all held amounts within 30 days of the triggering event. The following factors may result in denial of verified-Helper status. OxSteed evaluates applications based on the nature, severity, and recency of any criminal history. Not all disqualifying factors are permanent:

- Violent crimes (e.g., murder, aggravated assault);
- Sexual offenses (e.g., sexual assault, child sexual abuse, indecent exposure);
- Any felony within the past 7 years;
- Active registration on any sex-offender registry;
- Theft, burglary, or robbery within the past 5 years;
- Drug trafficking or distribution (any timeline);

## 10. Dispute Resolution

OxSteed provides a structured dispute-resolution process to handle disagreements between Customers and Helpers fairly and efficiently. The goal is resolution within 30 days where possible. This Section applies to transactions processed through OxSteed Pay; off-platform transactions are not covered.

### 10.1 Refund Window

Customers have **30 days** from the date a job is marked complete to file a dispute or refund request. After this window closes, payments are considered final. Exceptions may be granted for fraud, identity theft, or safety incidents - contact support@oxsteed.com.

### 10.2 Categories of Dispute

- **Quality Issues** - work that does not meet the agreed scope, is incomplete, or falls below professional standards;
- **Incomplete Work** - the Helper abandoned the job or failed to complete agreed deliverables without cause;
- **Property Damage or Injury** - physical damage to Customer property or personal injury during job execution;
- **Payment Discrepancy** - charges in excess of the agreed amount, unauthorized charges, or duplicate billing;
- **Security or Privacy Incident** - unauthorized access to Customer data, property, or accounts by a Helper;
- **Other** - any dispute not covered above; describe the issue in the ticket.

### 10.3 Resolution Process

1. **File a ticket** through the support portal. Include the job ID, date, description of the issue, and supporting evidence (photos, messages, third-party estimates).
2. **Review and evidence period.** Both parties are notified and have 5 business days to submit evidence or a response.
3. **Mediation.** An OxSteed Trust & Safety specialist reviews all evidence and attempts to reach a mutually agreeable resolution.
4. **Decision.** A binding decision is issued within 21 days. Refunds or adjustments are processed within 5 business days of the decision.
5. **Senior review (optional).** Either party may request escalation to a senior specialist within 5 days of the initial decision.
6. **Formal arbitration (optional).** If unsatisfied with senior review, either party may proceed to binding arbitration under Section 12.

A Helper who fails to respond within the 5-day evidence window may have the dispute decided in the Customer's favor based on the available evidence.

> **Warning:** Submitting false or misleading evidence in a dispute is a violation of these Terms and may result in account suspension.

### 10.4 Relationship to Chargebacks

The chargeback rules in Section 7.10 apply. Filing a chargeback without first attempting resolution through this dispute process is a violation of these Terms and may result in account suspension.

### 10.5 Dispute Fees

## 12. Binding Arbitration and Class Action Waiver

### 12.1 Agreement to Arbitrate

Any dispute between you and OxSteed arising from these Terms, your use of the platform, or any service provided will be resolved through **binding arbitration** rather than in court. By using OxSteed you agree that disputes will be resolved by a neutral arbitrator, not a judge or jury. This Section does not affect disputes between Customers and Helpers, which are governed by Section 10.

### 12.2 Arbitration Rules and Procedures

- Arbitration follows the rules of **JAMS** (Judicial Arbitration and Mediation Services) or **AAA** (American Arbitration Association), whichever is mutually chosen by the parties.
- Arbitration is conducted by a single, neutral arbitrator with experience in contract disputes - typically a retired judge or experienced attorney trained in dispute resolution and bound by confidentiality and conflict-of-interest disclosure.
- The arbitrator will issue a written decision explaining the reasoning. The decision is final and binding; appeal rights are limited to those provided by JAMS/AAA rules and applicable law.

### 12.3 Typical Timeline

1. **Initial Demand.** The claimant files a demand for arbitration, outlining the dispute and relief sought.
2. **Arbitrator Selection.** Both parties participate in selecting an arbitrator who then schedules the hearing.
3. **Hearing and Evidence.** The arbitrator hears evidence, testimony, and arguments from both parties - similar to a trial but more informal.
4. **Decision.** The arbitrator issues a written award.

### 12.4 Fees and Costs

- OxSteed will pay all JAMS or AAA filing and administrative fees if your claim is for under $10,000. For claims of $10,000 or more, costs are split per JAMS/AAA rules.
- Each party is responsible for its own attorney fees and costs unless the arbitrator awards attorney fees to the prevailing party (which is rare).
- For low-value claims (under $10,000), JAMS offers streamlined procedures that reduce costs significantly.

### 12.5 Class Action Waiver

Arbitration will be conducted on an **individual basis only.** You cannot bring a class action lawsuit against OxSteed, and OxSteed cannot bring a class action against you. All claims must be resolved individually.

### 12.6 Exceptions to Arbitration

This arbitration agreement does not apply to:

- **Small Claims Court** - either party may pursue an individual claim in small claims court instead of arbitration;
- **Injunctive or Equitable Relief** - either party may seek temporary restraining orders or injunctions in court to prevent irreparable harm;
- **Validity Challenges** - disputes about the validity of this arbitration agreement may be resolved by arbitration or by a court;
- **Regulatory Agencies** - claims brought by government agencies or regulatory bodies are not subject to arbitration.

### 12.7 State-Specific Enforceability

- **California.** California law permits class-action waivers in most consumer contracts; however, California Consumers Legal Remedies Act (CLRA) claims and certain wage-and-hour claims may not be subject to arbitration. California residents have the right to opt out of arbitration by providing written notice to legal@oxsteed.com within 30 days of first accepting these Terms.
- **New York.** Class-action waivers are generally enforceable in arbitration agreements in New York, but specific consumer-protection claims under New York General Business Law §349 (deceptive practices) may not be waivable through arbitration. New York residents may request arbitration to take place in New York County.
- **Massachusetts.** Massachusetts courts scrutinize arbitration waivers closely. Class-action waivers may not be enforceable in Massachusetts for certain consumer claims without explicit, clear acknowledgement. To the extent any provision is unenforceable under Massachusetts law, the remaining provisions of this Section continue in effect.

### 12.8 How to Initiate Arbitration

1. **Send Notice.** Send a detailed written notice to legal@oxsteed.com (with a copy to support@oxsteed.com) describing the dispute, relief sought, and proposed solution. Include dates, amounts, and supporting documentation.
2. **Wait for Response.** OxSteed will respond within 30 days. Many disputes are resolved during this negotiation period.
3. **File Demand.** If unresolved, file a demand for arbitration with JAMS or AAA following their procedures and submitting the filing fee (which OxSteed will reimburse if you prevail).
4. **Attend Hearing.** Participate in the arbitration. You may represent yourself, hire an attorney, or use other representation.
