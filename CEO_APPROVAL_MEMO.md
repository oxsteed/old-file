## SITUATION

We need legal framework (ToS, Privacy Policy, Agreements, Data Procedures) before June 1 launch. Current plan: Hire external counsel to do 100% of work.

**Cost:** $50-90K external counsel (150-280 billable hours)  
**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks

## PROPOSAL

I conduct research-and-draft phase internally, leveraging published legal frameworks from comparable marketplaces (TaskRabbit, Nextdoor, Andi, OfferUp, Roadie). External counsel does compliance review only.

**Cost:** $13-16K external counsel (50-60 billable hours)  
**Timeline:** 3 weeks internal + 2 weeks counsel review = 5 weeks total  
**Savings:** $37-74K (65-75% reduction)

## WHY THIS WORKS

1. **Comparable Platforms Have Published Their Frameworks**
   - TaskRabbit, Nextdoor, Andi, OfferUp, Roadie all have public Terms of Service
   - These have been reviewed by sophisticated counsel and tested in litigation
   - Their structures are available to research and learn from

2. **Compliance Language is Standardized**
   - GDPR/CCPA compliance language is largely boilerplate (all companies use similar language)
   - FLSA and FCRA have established best practices
   - Stripe payment procedures are well-documented

3. **I'm Drafting 90%, Not 100%**
   - I research and draft comprehensive frameworks
   - External counsel does compliance audit, jurisdiction-specific review, risk assessment
   - External counsel provides protection/liability shield if legal issues arise

4. **Internal Expertise Exists and is Employed**
   - I have legal knowledge and can research industry standards
   - Using internal staff for commodity legal work (research, drafting) is economical
   - Reserving external counsel for specialized work (employment law, risk assessment, compliance sign-off)

5. **External Counsel Still Reviews Everything**
   - Zero legal documents go live without external counsel approval
   - Counsel can still catch issues, identify gaps, require additional work
   - Risk is managed; we're not trying to avoid counsel, just reduce their time on foundational work

## RISK ASSESSMENT

- **Mitigation:** External counsel does full compliance audit; no documents go live without review
- **Residual Risk:** Low (counsel still reviews everything)

**Risk 2: Industry standards have changed since research**
- **Mitigation:** Research conducted in early May (most current); counsel final review confirms
- **Residual Risk:** Low (compliance guidance updates are infrequent)

**Risk 3: Drafts lack jurisdiction-specific customization**
- **Mitigation:** Counsel review specifically checks CA AB5, NY, MA requirements
- **Residual Risk:** Low (counsel is expert in jurisdictions)

**Risk 4: Internal team runs out of time**
- **Mitigation:** 91 hours ÷ 5 weeks = ~18 hours/week (part-time, not full-time); feasible
- **Residual Risk:** Low (scope is realistic)

## ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED

**Alternative 1: 100% External Counsel (Status Quo)**
- Pros: Full expert review from day one
- Cons: $50-90K cost; longer timeline; redundant research (counsel will research same sources)
- Verdict: Too expensive; wastes counsel's hourly rate on commodity work

**Alternative 2: 50/50 Internal-External Split**
- Pros: Moderate cost reduction; shared risk
- Cons: Unclear ownership; higher coordination overhead; counsel still does ~50% of foundation work
- Verdict: Less optimal than this proposal; creates ambiguity

**Alternative 3: This Proposal (Internal Research + External Review)**
- Pros: Maximum cost savings; leverages internal expertise; counsel focuses on compliance/risk; clear ownership
- Cons: Requires internal team commitment; counsel doesn't do foundation research (acceptable because frameworks are industry-standard)
- Verdict: Optimal approach; balances cost, quality, and timeline
